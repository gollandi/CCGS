# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
CCGS consultants receiving an invitation on phones and desktop browsers.

## Purpose
An invitation to the Annual Autumn Event: Wednesday 4 November 2026, 19:00, No. Fifty Cheyne, 50 Cheyne Walk, Chelsea, London SW3 5LR. CONSULTANT ONLY. Celebrating what we've built together.

## Success
Essential event information is immediately accessible. Visitors can enjoy a complete still composition, then discover a consequential spatial transformation and a surprising sourced story. Artistic conviction and surprise require human judgement; the implementation alone does not establish them.

## Binding direction
The latest owner brief preserves V2 as the first iteration worth retaining. It supersedes the earlier replacement mandate: evolve the existing experience, do not restart it. Objects should become events or relationships. Preserve the flyer through warmth, natural light, intimacy and mischievous humour, not literal imagery. No serif, rigid bow, floating translucent ribbons, perpetual decorative motion, dashboard cards or illustrative medical metaphors.

The current scope is one wine-refraction vertical slice on `feat/refraction-art-slice`. A continuous, open transparent membrane refracts the restaurant photograph. Press and drag indent it locally; dragging does not open the wine story. A tap or keyboard activation opens the complete story directly. No isolated “Not” appears. The owner explicitly liked this effect on the restaurant background on 21 October 2026 and then requested only better portal placement. Preserve the effect. The three secondary portals follow its lower curve in reading order: chef, dogs, neighbourhood. Each story includes a sourced photograph or contextual map and its credit. Keep the four story routes; the research catalogue is separate from the interface.

## Constraints
One viewport in ordinary use; retain compact internal reading scroll and extreme narrow-width reflow as accessibility escapes. Touch and keyboard access, reduced motion, static non-WebGL fallback and essential information before JavaScript. No invented RSVP endpoint, menu, wine selection, attendance, photography or factual claims. RSVP asks for a reply in the Consultants WhatsApp chat, without a link. Work on the dedicated branch; no merge into main without review and no automatic public deployment.

Discovery sources and limits remain in docs/DISCOVERIES.md; the approved flyer supplies event facts, not current restaurant menu evidence.
