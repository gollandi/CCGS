# One transparent fold

20 October 2026. Local revision on `feat/refraction-art-slice`.

The owner accepted the principle of subtraction but rejected the broken-line result. The supplied concept image is now the explicit material reference: a more minimal transparent surface, with an emphatic no-serif constraint. This supersedes the line experiment and the earlier blanket exclusion of a translucent surface, while preserving the instruction not to copy the mockup literally.

The current shader draws one broad face, a crease, a fine perimeter and restrained reflected light. Its ends extend beyond the artwork frame. Pointer input deforms the crease and edges differently. It has no photograph, gold sculpture, visible circular lens or large idle word. First/second activation, the sourced wine story, other discoveries, event facts, calendar and RSVP explanation remain. The static fallback uses an SVG surface approximation. Motion remains finite and rendered on demand.

Observed validation: production build, TypeScript and whitespace checks pass. Desktop and mobile browser captures show the surface and preserve the essential information. The browser's computed font-family check across headings, prose, links, buttons, summaries, footer, date and venue returned only `Manrope, sans-serif`, including the open story. The mobile WebGL renderer was observed ready. No new claims are made about physical iPhone compatibility, optical simulation accuracy or artistic approval.

Evidence is saved in the chat outputs as ccgs-fold-desktop.jpg, ccgs-fold-mobile.jpg and ccgs-fold-mobile-story.jpg. Nothing is merged to main or publicly deployed.
