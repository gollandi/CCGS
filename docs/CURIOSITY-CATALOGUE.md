# No. Fifty Cheyne — curiosity catalogue

Research inventory, 21 October 2026. This is the complete candidate list extracted from the sources consulted for this project, including the earlier discovery research. It is not a claim to exhaust everything written about the restaurant. The invitation still contains four stories; this catalogue is an editorial selection bank, not extra interface content.

**OBSERVED** means the named source contains the claim. Historical reporting remains historical; it is not confirmation of present-day service. None of the dishes, wines, rooms or dog arrangements below is a promise about the CCGS dinner. The owner specifies Wednesday 4 November 2026 at 19:00, with replies in the Consultants WhatsApp chat.

## Recommended shortlist

Keep the wine name, the accidental chef, dogs with beds and Rossetti’s wombat. The most promising additions are the fourteen-course job interview (historical verification first), books and cocktails, the jazz/theatre connection, the seasonally changing pie and the Ruby Bar. These are editorial recommendations, not approved additions to the four-story interface.

## Excluded or unresolved material

- **Reddit:** the earlier exact-name search found a wedding recommendation and subjective lighting comment in [U]. The 21 October exact-name query returned no additional results. Neither establishes a worthwhile factual story; no Reddit anecdote has been fabricated.
- **Weddings:** deliberately excluded by the owner. Not a discovery candidate for this invitation.
- **Old chef listings:** brochures still naming Iain Smith are historical. The current restaurant interview identifies Steven Connolly.
- **Room capacities:** official pages and brochures differ. They are operational details requiring venue confirmation, not decorative facts.
- **Specific celebrity diners:** generic marketing mentions are insufficient to name people or imply attendance.
- **Food and wine availability:** sample lists and dated reviews do not establish what will be served at this dinner.
- **Photos:** three photographs and one contextual map are locally stored, visually inspected and individually recorded in `public/images/chelsea/manifest.json`. The Dog Day image is used by the 2026 event page; its capture date is unknown. The map does not depict Rossetti’s house specifically. Website availability does not establish an open reuse licence.
