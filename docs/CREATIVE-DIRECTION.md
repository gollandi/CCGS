# Creative direction — approved decisions and rejected paths

## North star
The first impression: half a second of curiosity, then stillness and surprise. The user must immediately understand **what / when / where**, want to attend, want to explore, and discover surprising true facts. This is an intimate celebration of colleagues' shared achievements, not an institutional campaign.

**Emotional line (approved):** “Celebrating what we've built together.”

## Approved visual family
Based on the approved vertical autumn dinner flyer: warm ivory, honey/amber autumn light, Chelsea riverside, rich burnt foliage, a sophisticated and slightly playful dinner atmosphere, precise high-contrast lettering. Its name is **Annual Autumn Event**, not “Let's pretend we're a family” (that was a concept, never copy). The stamp reads **CONSULTANT ONLY**. RSVP replaces guest-count copy. Approved venue/date: No. Fifty Cheyne, 4 November 2026, 19:00.

The later wide mockup introduced a flowing **translucent ribbon/membrane** that refracts Chelsea sunset imagery. The user approved this concept **but requested extreme subtraction, higher contrast, stronger hierarchy, and hidden mischievous discoveries**. Treat that later mockup as compositional direction, not a pixel-perfect template.

## The form
- Purely digital, impossible transformations; **not** a physical surface, planet, globe, honeycomb, or Bohr atom.
- The membrane is a **living object with a purpose and destiny**: its state changes when touched, and it reveals real content through refraction/masking. Avoid idle screensaver motion.
- Single viewport; **no page scroll**. Discovery via drag/tap and spatial transitions, never conventional vertical sections.
- Preserve spaciousness, near-empty composition, controlled asymmetry and one deliberate primary focal point.
- Practical details are fixed, legible, and accessible at all times, including on mobile.
- Curiosities are quiet, unexpected and rewarding, never a grid of cards/pills.
- Typographic contrast must survive pale background and luminous photographic treatment.
- Distinct motion phases: entrance / idle / touch / discovery / dismiss / return. Calm, controlled, responsive.
- Prefer a compelling static composition before motion loads. No invisible content dependent on IntersectionObserver or JS; a previous prototype showed an empty white screen on iPhone.

## Anti-patterns — explicitly rejected
- Navy + terracotta + monumental serif + spaced micro-labels used as generic “AI editorial luxury” cliché.
- Excessive typography or decorative labels; Claude/ChatGPT visual slop.
- Stock futuristic atom/orbits/science iconography; no unearned metaphors of transition.
- Trans/gender imagery used as simplistic transition symbolism, or patients' lives used as decoration.
- Six cards, frosted glass dashboard, pointless orbiting dots, faux interactions.
- A scroll-driven one-pager; an inaccessible interactive puzzle; overlaid text losing contrast in the image.
- Artificially sentimental “family” headline or a corporate three-word footer.

## Narrative principle
A group of surgeons chose to work together on historically underserved care, outside the expected clinical orthodoxy. The event is a celebration of their **shared work and companionship**. Suggest the team's originality through interaction, not sloganising or exploiting patient stories.

## Desired result
It should feel unmistakably related to the approved invitation while becoming an unprecedented digital object. Beauty by removal; surprise by transformation.
