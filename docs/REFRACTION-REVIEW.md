# Refraction — one artistic vertical slice

20 October 2026. Local implementation on `feat/refraction-art-slice`. Not merged to main, not publicly deployed, not a claim of creative acceptance.

## Audit and preservation

V2 is the foundation worth preserving: the warm paper ground, locally hosted sans serif typography, immediately available event details, four sourced curiosities, reversible gestures, keyboard focus restoration, truthful RSVP explanation and calendar export. Its weak point was representational motion: objects and a continuously moving ribbon illustrated their subjects without changing what the visitor could perceive.

The owner explicitly asked for refinement, not replacement, and for one complete transformation before expanding the others. The wine discovery is that slice. Chef, dogs and Rossetti remain working discoveries with their original facts and sources.

## Three interpretations and critique

| Interpretation | Event | Strength | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Refraction | A line loses continuity inside an optical field; a distorted word becomes part of the sentence the visitor discovers. | The same visible material becomes useful language. A small gesture can change the meaning of the scene. | An ornamental lens or an unrelated content panel would break the premise. |
| Topological cut | The dog-tag ring makes two separated parts of the scene briefly adjacent. | Can turn spatial relationships into the discovery mechanism. | Easily becomes a familiar portal effect; the dog story could feel attached after the fact. |
| Impossible herbarium | A leaf reveals geometry larger than its own silhouette, with the neighbourhood story inside. | Strong connection to the existing object and unexpected history. | Expansion could overwhelm the invitation or substitute spectacular geometry for meaning. |

Refraction was selected because it connects the optical character of glass with the mischievous wine name already present in the source material. It needs one line and one word, rather than additional objects or fictional content.

## Implemented consequence

The field is initially usable. A finite optical score changes the line and word, then settles. Pointer intention increases refraction; small tremors do not trigger disclosure. Clicking, keyboard activation or a deliberate pull opens the discovery. The visible `lunch.` travels and scales into its measured place in `Not the ladies who lunch.`. At rest, the semantic heading takes over so scrolling and text layout remain dependable. The other three discovery anchors move to the edge of the opened space.

The heading has its complete accessible name throughout the transfer. Essential event details do not participate in distortion. Returning restores the optical word and focus to the discovery button.

The shader renders on demand and stops requesting frames after convergence. Its finite score is measured in active renderer time after texture readiness, not a certified wall-clock performance budget. The DOM word has a finite authored sequence. No continuous breathing, floating or spinning loops remain active.

Removed from this slice: the procedural ribbon, instructional exploration hint, ornamental plus markers, the extra invitation slogan, background light gradients and the explanatory subtitle inside the lens. Original reference assets and historical implementation records remain intact.

## Review findings and corrections

An independent source and screenshot review found three substantive issues in the first pass:

1. Pausing during the reveal could preserve partial clipping. GSAP now reverts its animation context; a browser interruption check returned `clip-path: none` and opacity 1 for body, aside and source links.
2. The static fallback appeared behind the working renderer during reading. Wine-open now hides that fallback and the fallback no longer contains a duplicate word.
3. Optical text disappeared before an unrelated heading entered. The word now travels into the sentence before the semantic destination takes over.

The bounded confirmation accepted the continuity structure and identified the temporary incomplete accessible heading. A permanent `aria-label` supplies the complete story title, verified in the browser during the transfer. The independent reviewer did not watch the animation or certify subjective surprise.

## Verification and evidence

- Production build and TypeScript checks pass; seven targeted gesture/score tests pass.
- Chrome: four discoveries in 1440×900, 390×844, 375×667, 320×568 and 844×390. No document overflow or overlap between reading region and event details in this set. Compact reading regions scroll internally.
- Deliberate drag opens wine; Escape closes and restores the wine button's focus.
- Pause during reveal restores complete readable content. The paused composition uses the static path.
- Safari desktop and Safari Responsive Design Mode at 390×844 show the wine transformation and readable settled result. This is WebKit evidence, not physical iPhone evidence. The Simulator application could not be accessed in this environment.
- Existing facts, source URLs, RSVP disclosure and calendar file are preserved. No submission endpoint or event wine selection has been invented.

Visual captures and the browser-check record are delivered in the chat outputs folder under the `ccgs-refraction-` prefix. Static images show composition and endpoints; they cannot demonstrate timing, touch feel or GPU performance.

## Eight-question evaluation

| Question | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Is the event immediately understandable? | Yes in the observed desktop and mobile compositions: title, date, time, venue, audience and response details remain exposed. |
| Does the first screen produce surprise? | It contains an optical discontinuity and a finite transformation. Whether this produces the requested emotional arrest remains a human judgement. |
| Is there a genuine reason to interact? | The displaced word and broken line invite investigation; activation rewards that investigation with a sourced, mischievous wine name. |
| Does interaction transform the experience? | The optical word becomes the sentence and the other anchors change position. It is no longer only a decorative object followed by a panel. |
| Are discoveries intellectually rewarding? | The existing wine-name, chef, dog and Rossetti curiosities retain their sources and factual limits. Their interest is subjective; no new claims were added. |
| Is it related to the flyer? | Warmth, restraint, transparency and a shared evening remain. Its composition and imagery are not copied. |
| Could it be mistaken for a generic luxury landing page? | The static reading state still uses familiar typography, but the living line-to-language relationship is specific to this discovery. No luxury materials, stock imagery or ornamental ribbon remain in the active field. |
| Does every visible element deserve to exist? | Event facts enable attendance; three retained anchors preserve discoveries; the line and word perform the wine transformation; pause and source links preserve control and trust. The redundant subtitle and instructional decorations were removed. |

The other three visual laws have deliberately not been expanded. This slice is ready for the owner's artistic review; a claim of masterpiece status or physical-device reliability would exceed the evidence.
